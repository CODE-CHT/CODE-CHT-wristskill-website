Lotl the axolotl scored a slushie truck. He needs a partner to run it. You in?

Real weather. Real money. Your decisions. A business game for kids 8–14, inspired by the classic 1990s classroom business sims.

Download on the App Store

Each morning, read the weather and the town schedule, stock the truck, set your prices and pick your stops. The day's report shows what sold, what melted, what gas cost, and who you missed.

For grown-ups

  • What's free, and what isn't. Today's Route — a fresh daily challenge — is free every day. New players also get one free practice weekend (a short 3-day route). The Full Summer — the 14- and 28-day routes, plus the weekend route as often as you like — is a one-time purchase through the App Store. Nothing to renew or cancel.
  • A grown-up stays in charge of buying. A grown-up question appears before the purchase screen, and families can use Ask to Buy.
  • Nothing to sign up for, nothing collected. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Progress saves only on the device.
  • The math is real. Costs, prices, weather, and profit add up honestly — and a bad day is a lesson, never a punishment.
