For grown-ups

  • What's free, and what isn't. Today's Route — a fresh daily challenge, the same day for everyone playing — is free, every day, no strings. New players also get one free practice weekend (a short 3-day route). The full summer — the 14- and 28-day routes — is a one-time unlock: buy a code, type it into the game once, and it works forever, even offline. No subscription, and never a price or a checkout on the kid's screen — the game tells them to ask a grown-up.
  • Nothing to sign up for, nothing collected. No ads, no accounts, no tracking — progress saves only on this device. Checkout happens on our store page, never inside the game.
  • Progress stays in this browser. The summer saves itself after every day, in this browser on this device — there is no account and no copy anywhere to restore from. Clearing your browser's cookies and site data erases it, and a private or incognito window forgets everything when it closes.
  • Works where kids are. Phones and tablets in landscape, plus laptops and Chromebooks — any modern browser.
  • The math is real. Costs, prices, weather, and profit add up honestly — and a bad day is a lesson, never a punishment.
  • On iPhone and iPad, add it to the Home Screen. Open the game, then choose Add to Home Screen in the share menu. It installs with its own icon and works offline — and it is the one way to keep a long summer safe, because Safari deletes a website's stored data after about seven days of not visiting it. A Home Screen app is exempt from that.
