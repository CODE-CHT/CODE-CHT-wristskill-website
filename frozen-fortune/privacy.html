Privacy Policy

Frozen Fortune · Last updated [PUBLISH DATE]

Frozen Fortune is a slushie-truck business game for kids, made by WristSkill Labs LLC.

We don't collect any information

The game has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't ask for a name, email, age or location. It doesn't use the camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

Your progress stays on your device

Game progress, records, settings, the money words your kid has collected and a history of past daily routes are saved only on the device the game is played on. They aren't sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them. If your device makes its own backups (for example iCloud Backup), those backups may include this game data; that's between you and Apple.

Purchases are handled by Apple

The one-time Full Summer unlock is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we don't see payment details or your Apple Account. A grown-up question appears before the purchase screen, and families can use Ask to Buy.

Sharing

The game can copy a short text result (emoji and a score) for you to paste yourself. It contains no personal information and the game doesn't send it anywhere.

What Apple shows us

Apple gives app makers anonymous, combined App Store statistics, such as how many people downloaded the game, and crash reports from people who chose to share them in their device settings. These don't identify anyone, and you can turn the sharing off in your device's privacy settings.

Children

The game is made for kids. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
