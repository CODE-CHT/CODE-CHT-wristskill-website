Privacy Policy

The short version

Frozen Fortune is a business game for kids 8–14. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. A player's progress is saved on their own device, and the game doesn't send it to us.

This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app and the version that plays in a web browser at wristskill.com/frozen-fortune.

What the game keeps on the device

  • Game progress: the current day, money, stock, prices, routes played and personal records
  • Today's Route results and the streak of days played
  • Settings, such as sound on or off
  • Whether the full summer is unlocked, and the unlock code if one was typed in

That is the whole list. In the app it sits in the app's own storage, and deleting the app deletes it. In a browser it sits in that browser's storage for wristskill.com, and clearing the site's data deletes it.

What we don't collect

  • Names, emails, ages, birthdays, photos, voice or location from players
  • Advertising IDs or any identifier for a device or a player
  • Anything typed or played in the game

There are no ads in the game, and no analytics or tracking code in the game. We don't share or sell anything about players, because the game doesn't send us anything about them.

Buying the full summer

Today's Route is free. The full summer is a one-time purchase. It does not renew, and there is nothing to cancel.

In the app, Apple handles the payment. We never see a card, an Apple Account or a name. Apple tells us that a sale happened, not who made it. The buy screen sits behind a grown-up question, and Apple's own payment sheet asks the account holder to confirm. On a child's account with Ask to Buy, the family organizer approves the purchase first. Refunds go through Apple.

On the web, a grown-up buys an unlock code from our checkout partner, Lemon Squeezy, which handles the payment and emails the code. As the seller, we can see the buyer's name, email address and order details, and we use them only to deliver the code and answer questions about the order. Lemon Squeezy has its own privacy policy.

Either way, the unlock is checked on the device itself. The game doesn't contact us to check a code, so we don't learn who typed one in or where.

Sharing a result

After Today's Route, a player can copy or share a short result card: the date, a row of emoji for the day's stops, the day's score and a link to this game. It only leaves the device if the player presses the button and chooses where to send it. We aren't told that it was shared.

The web version

The browser version is hosted on Netlify, and the web pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Like most websites, those services receive the visitor's IP address and browser details when the page loads. We don't add tracking to the game page.

Children's privacy

Frozen Fortune is made for children, so it was built to collect nothing from them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only personal information we receive is a web buyer's order details, which come from the grown-up making the purchase. A parent with a question, or who thinks we hold something about their child, can email us and we will answer and delete it.

Changes

This page describes the game as it is today. If we ever start collecting something we don't collect now, we will update this page and change the date at the top before that starts.
