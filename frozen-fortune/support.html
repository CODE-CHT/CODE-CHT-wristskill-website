Help

Frozen Fortune for iPhone and iPad

How do I play?

Each morning, read the weather and the town schedule, stock the truck, set your prices and pick your stops. The report at the end of the day shows what sold, what melted, what gas cost, and how many customers you missed or priced away.

What's free?

Today's Route is free every day, and new players get one free 3-day practice weekend.

What does the Full Summer purchase include?

The 14-day and 28-day routes, plus the weekend route as often as you like. It's a one-time purchase, with nothing to renew or cancel.

I bought it on another device or a family member bought it

Open the game, tap "More routes", then "For grown-ups", answer the grown-up question, then tap "Restore a purchase". Family Sharing is on for this purchase.

My child's purchase says "Sent to your family organizer."

With Ask to Buy, the organizer approves the request on their device. The summer opens by itself once they say yes.

Can I turn off sounds or vibration?

Yes: open Settings and tap the Sound or Vibration button.

How do I hear Lotl's tips again?

Open Settings and tap "Hear Lotl's tips again". They come back on the next first day. "Watch the story again" replays the opening story. Handy when a brother or sister starts playing on the same device.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on this device. Deleting the app deletes it. Your device's own backups (like iCloud Backup) may include it.
