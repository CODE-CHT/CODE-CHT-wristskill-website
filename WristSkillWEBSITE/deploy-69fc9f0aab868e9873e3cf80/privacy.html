Privacy policy

Your data is stored on your device unless you choose to share it.

Last updated May 7, 2026.

Data collection

WristSkill™ does not collect personal data for analytics, advertising, or developer access. Performance data generated by the app is stored locally on the user's device unless the user chooses to share progress from the app.

Data storage

Session results, baseline scores, and session history are saved to the device's local storage. This data is not accessible to the developer or to any third party unless the user intentionally uses the app's progress-sharing option.

Progress sharing

WristSkill includes an option to share progress. Sharing is initiated by the user and may send selected progress information through the sharing method the user chooses, such as a message, email, or another app on the device.

Third-party services

WristSkill does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or developer-operated data collection services. The app may check online for a new version notification at launch; this request transmits no personal data. If the user chooses to share progress, the selected sharing destination may process that information according to its own privacy practices.

Children's privacy

WristSkill is intended for use by hand therapy clinicians and patients with wrist injuries. While the app is available to users of all ages, it is not specifically directed to children under 13. WristSkill does not collect personal data from users, regardless of age. Progress information is shared only when a user intentionally uses the app's sharing option.

Medical disclaimer

WristSkill is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition and does not provide medical advice. Performance data generated by the app should not be used as a substitute for professional clinical evaluation or treatment planning.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Material changes — including any future addition of advertising, analytics, or in-app purchase services — will be reflected on this page before those changes take effect in the app. Continued use of WristSkill after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact WristSkillapp@gmail.com.
