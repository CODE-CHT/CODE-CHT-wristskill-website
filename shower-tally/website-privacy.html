Website Privacy Notice

WristSkill Labs, LLC · Effective September 14, 2026

This notice covers the Shower Tally pages of this website: the blog, the free printable shower chart, and the optional email signup. The Shower Tally app has its own privacy policy, and nothing on this page changes it. The app still does not ask for your email address.

What these pages collect automatically

No analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies run on these pages. Two services see standard technical information when you visit, because a web page cannot load without them:

  • Netlify, our website host, processes requests to serve and secure the site, which includes your IP address and browser details. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface, so your browser requests it from Google, which receives your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

Downloading the free printable does not require any information from you.

The optional email signup

If you choose to enter your email address, we collect that address and the page you signed up from. We use it only to send occasional short tips on hygiene routines and news about Shower Tally. The signup is stored with Netlify. If we later use an email service to send messages, we will name it here before any address is moved to it.

  • We never sell or rent your email address, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.
  • Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • We keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. To have it deleted, email WristSkillapp@gmail.com and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

The signup is for parents, caregivers, and other adults. These pages are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect a child's email address. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this notice changes, the effective date above will change with it. Questions or requests: WristSkillapp@gmail.com. Shower Tally is published by WristSkill Labs, LLC, a United States company.
