Questions

Is Shower Tally really free?

Yes. You can track a family and log showers for free, forever. Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks unlimited members, custom schedules, and pets.

What devices does it work on?

Shower Tally is available now on iPhone via the App Store. An Android version is on the way.

Does everyone in the family need the app?

Anyone with the app can log showers for the whole household, so a shared board works even if only one parent has it installed. For personal nudges, each person installs it and joins the family.

Is my family's data private?

Your data is only visible to members of your family. We don't sell it. See our Privacy Policy for details.
