Course

Edema Management of the Upper Extremity

  • Live on Zoom or on-site
  • About 3 hours
  • 4 learning objectives

Practical, evidence-based edema management for orthopedic conditions of the hand and upper extremity. Offered live on Zoom, or booked for your clinic, school or group.

Learning objectives

By the end of the course, learners will be able to:

  1. Differentiate inflammatory, subacute and chronic types of edema.
  2. Examine the risks and benefits of coban finger taping for edema reduction.
  3. Demonstrate manual edema mobilization techniques.
  4. Execute kinesiology taping patterns and discuss low-stretch wrapping for lymphatic flow.

What it covers

  • Prevention and assessment of edema
  • Manual edema mobilization vs. lymphatic drainage
  • Measurement techniques
  • Intervention strategies and practical application of MEM
  • Kinesiology taping and wrapping

Where it's been taught

Taught in person in Indianapolis, Indiana (March 2025) and in Illinois (November 2025). The in-person course runs about three hours.
