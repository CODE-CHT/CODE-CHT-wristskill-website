Questions

Is there a free version or a free trial?

No. The app is a paid product with no free trial — $0.99/month, $9.99/year, or $30 once for lifetime. One thing is always free: the Health & Safety screen, with crisis and eating-disorder support resources, is readable by anyone without paying.

Does the app tell me how much protein to eat?

No, and that's deliberate. The app never calculates, suggests, or recommends a protein amount. You pick your own daily number — ideally with a physician, dietitian, or other qualified health provider — and Jack Steel just tracks it.

What happens to my data?

Everything you log stays on your phone. There are no accounts and no servers, no ads, and no data sale. The app sends only anonymous usage counts that carry no identity and none of your numbers. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Who is the app for?

Ages 13 and up. It's a general-wellness habit tracker made for gym culture — not a medical device, and it makes no claims about your health. It deliberately has no weight, calorie, or body-composition tracking, no photos, and no leaderboards. If you're under 18, set your goal with a parent or guardian and a qualified health provider.

Is it on Android?

Not yet — Jack Steel's Protein Tracker is currently iPhone-only.
