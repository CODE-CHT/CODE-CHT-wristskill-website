Common questions

How do I set or change my daily protein goal?

Open the settings sheet (gear icon) and edit your goal. The app never calculates or recommends an amount — pick your number with a physician, dietitian, or other qualified health provider.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple and managed in your App Store account: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or the MANAGE link in the app's settings sheet. The Lifetime option is a one-time purchase — it does not renew and there is nothing to cancel.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Open the app and tap Restore Purchases on the plan screen while signed into the same Apple Account.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. There are no accounts — your log history never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes your data. The app sends only anonymous usage counts (no identity, no amounts); the privacy policy has the full detail.
