Privacy policy

Your data stays on your device. Anonymous analytics help us improve the app.

Last updated May 14, 2026.

Data collection

WristSkill™ uses Firebase (provided by Google) to collect anonymous usage analytics, including app sessions, feature usage, and crash reports. This data does not include names, email addresses, health records, or any information that identifies an individual. All performance data generated by the app is stored locally on the user's device.

Data storage

Session results, baseline scores, and session history are saved to the device's local storage and are not uploaded to any server. Anonymous usage analytics collected by Firebase are transmitted to Google's servers and governed by Google's privacy policy. No performance data or health information is included in analytics transmissions.

Sharing your results

WristSkill includes an optional share feature that uses your device's system share sheet. When you tap share, you choose where the data goes — for example, email, messages, the Files app, or AirDrop. WristSkill does not see, log, or retain a record of what you share or where you send it.

Third-party services

WristSkill uses Firebase (provided by Google) for anonymous crash reporting and usage analytics. Firebase may collect device identifiers and usage events automatically. No advertising networks or data brokers are used. The app may also check online for a version update notification at launch; this request transmits no personal data.

Children's privacy

WristSkill is intended for use by hand therapy clinicians and patients with wrist injuries. While the app is available to users of all ages, it is not specifically directed to children under 13. Because WristSkill does not collect or transmit any personal data, no information is gathered from any user, regardless of age.

Medical disclaimer

WristSkill is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition and does not provide medical advice. Performance data generated by the app should not be used as a substitute for professional clinical evaluation or treatment planning.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Material changes — including any future addition of advertising, analytics, or in-app purchase services — will be reflected on this page before those changes take effect in the app. Continued use of WristSkill after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact wristskillapp@gmail.com.
