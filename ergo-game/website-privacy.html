This notice covers the desk setup and movement-break game pages of this website, including the "Request a pilot" form. It is about these web pages only. The game itself is still in development and will have its own privacy policy.

What these pages collect automatically

No analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies run on these pages, and they load no outside fonts or scripts. Netlify, our website host, processes requests to serve and secure the site, which includes your IP address and browser details. See Netlify's privacy policy.

The pilot request form

If you send the form, we collect what you enter: your name, work email, company, number of employees, the chat tool your team uses, and any note you add. We use it only to reply to you about a pilot. The form is stored with Netlify.

Please don't include anyone's health information. The form doesn't ask for it, and we don't need it.

We don't sell or rent your details, and we don't share them for anyone else's marketing. We don't add you to a mailing list.

We keep your request until you ask us to delete it. To have it deleted, email admin@wristskill.com and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

These pages are for employers and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect a child's information. If you believe a child has sent the form, email us and we will delete it.
