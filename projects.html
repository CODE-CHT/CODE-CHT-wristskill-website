For the clinic

A gap existed in accessible, portable clinical measurement tools. I built to fill it.

WristSkill

iOS · Unity · C#
Live

Wrist proprioception tracking had no lightweight, phone-based option — just bulky lab equipment.

Wrist proprioception assessment for clinicians and patients. Standard goniometers measure angle — WristSkill measures accuracy. Tracks six motion planes per hand with session history and baseline promotion.

Unity 6 C# iOS Firebase
Download on App Store Read the clinical research →

AngleSnap

Chrome Extension · Vanilla JS
Live

Clinicians measuring range of motion on screen had no purpose-built tool — a physical goniometer can't measure a screen.

Measure joint angles on any image or video in your browser. Click three points — proximal, joint, distal — instant reading: 55° (125°). All points draggable, nothing stored.

Chrome MV3 Vanilla JS Free

For the house

My family needed a better system than a whiteboard.

Shower Tally

Live
iOS · Android · React Native

Every family has a shower rotation problem. Nobody admits it. The whiteboard always breaks down.

A family shower tracker — the digital whiteboard your household actually uses. Every family member gets a card: Fresh ✓, Due Soon, or Overdue. Cards sort by urgency and sync across all devices via Firebase.

"Clean or not, the family knows."

Web App · React + Vite
Live
