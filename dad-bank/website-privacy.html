Website Privacy Notice

WristSkill Labs, LLC · Effective September 14, 2026

This notice covers the Dad Bank pages of this website, including the optional email signup for launch updates. It is about the website only. The Dad Bank app has its own privacy policy, and nothing here changes it: the app has no accounts and never asks for your email address.

What these pages collect automatically

No analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies run on these pages, and they load no outside fonts or scripts. Netlify, our website host, processes requests to serve and secure the site, which includes your IP address and browser details. See Netlify's privacy policy.

The optional email signup

If you choose to enter your email address, we collect that address. We use it only to tell you when Dad Bank launches and to send occasional news about Dad Bank. The signup is stored with Netlify. If we later use an email service to send messages, we will name it here before any address is moved to it.

  • We never sell or rent your email address, and we don't share it for anyone else's marketing.
  • Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • We keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. To have it deleted, email admin@wristskill.com and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

The signup is for parents and other adults. These pages are written for parents and are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect a child's email address. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this notice changes, the effective date above will change with it. Questions or requests: admin@wristskill.com. Dad Bank is published by WristSkill Labs, LLC, a United States company.
