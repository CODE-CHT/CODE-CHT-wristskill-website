How it works

Their own pretend bank

Kids tap their picture and enter their PIN to see their balance, what they put in, what their money earned, and how close they are to their goal.

Money that makes money

Pick an interest rate, weekly or monthly, and Dad Bank adds it for them. Kids get to see what leaving money alone does.

They ask. You approve.

Kids ask to cash out, and you approve or decline. Allowance lands on payday by itself, and you can add a bonus any time.

The fine print, up front

  • No accounts, no servers. Your family's ledger lives on your phone. Backups go where you send them: a file you save, or your own iCloud or Google account.
  • No ads, no tracking. The app has no ads, no analytics and nothing that reports back to us.
  • Not a bank. Dad Bank never holds, moves or invests real money. It keeps score; the real money stays with you.
  • Free to start. The kid app is free to install. Dad Bank Plus is a one-time purchase on the parent's phone that adds pairing a kid's own phone, a "when can I afford it?" planner, and pretend borrowing with a cost you set.

FAQ

Is Dad Bank a real bank?

No. Dad Bank is not a bank. It never holds, moves or invests real money. You keep score in the app, and the real money stays with you.

Does my family need an account?

No. There are no accounts and no Dad Bank servers. The ledger lives on the parent's phone, and backups go to a file you save or your own iCloud or Google account.

Can my kid use their own phone?

Yes, with Dad Bank Plus on the parent's phone. The kid app is free to install. Their phone pairs with yours and syncs over your home Wi-Fi, encrypted.

When does Dad Bank launch?

Soon, on iPhone and Android. Leave your email and we'll let you know when it's out.
